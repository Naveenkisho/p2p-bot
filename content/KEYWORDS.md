Style notes for every article:
- 600–900 words, `##` sections, one `###` max per section.

- India-first language (rupees, UPI/IMPS, lakh/crore where natural).
- Facts only: what the desk actually does; tax/legal topics get a "talk to a
  professional" note, never advice.
