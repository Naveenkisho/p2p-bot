"Sell USDT" really means "convert USDT into money in my bank". The blockchain
half of that is the same for everyone — the difference between a good and bad
experience is the INR half: **how** the rupees reach your account. Here is how
each payout method works and when to use it.

## UPI — the default for speed

UPI transfers land in seconds, any hour, any day, into any UPI-enabled bank
account. For most orders it is the obvious choice. Practical notes:

- Best suited to small and medium orders — banks apply daily UPI limits per account.
- The payout arrives as a normal UPI credit from the desk's side; your bank app shows it instantly.
- Rate for UPI payouts is shown separately on the [live rate grid](/#rates) — methods are priced individually.

## IMPS — the workhorse for larger amounts

IMPS moves money bank-to-bank in near real time with higher per-transaction
ceilings than UPI, and it also works around the clock. If your order is too
large for comfortable UPI limits, IMPS is usually the right method. You
provide account number and IFSC on the [sell form](/sell), and the credit
lands directly in the account you named — which must be **your own** account
(third-party payouts are refused, per the [terms](/legal/terms), and that
rule protects you as much as the desk).

## CDM — cash deposited straight into your account

A CDM (cash deposit machine) payout means physical cash deposited over the
counter or by machine directly into your account. It suits sellers who want
cash-origin funds — deposits that enter the banking system at your own
branch. It is slower than UPI/IMPS (someone physically goes to a machine)
and rates reflect that cost difference honestly, up front.

## Cheque — the paper trail option

Cheque payouts are the slowest but leave the most traditional paper trail,
which some sellers specifically want for accounting. Ask support before
choosing it for a time-sensitive amount.

## Timing: what "instant" honestly means

Two clocks run in every order. The **blockchain clock**: your USDT transfer
confirms on-chain in seconds to a couple of minutes, and the scanner
verifies it automatically — the mechanics are covered in the
[step-by-step guide](/learn/how-to-sell-usdt-for-inr). The **payout clock**:
verified orders enter the payout queue and admins pay them in order. The
current typical time is shown live on the home page, and your
[order page](/my) shows your queue position — no "checking with the team"
messages, just the actual state.

## Which method should you pick?

- Under ~₹1 lakh equivalent: **UPI** — fastest, simplest.
- Larger amounts: **IMPS** — real-time with higher ceilings.
- Want cash-origin deposits: **CDM**.
- Want maximum paper trail and don't mind waiting: **cheque**.

Whatever you pick, the deeper safety question is the same: whose money is
being sent to you? That is covered by the
[clean-funds guarantee](/guarantee) and in detail in the
[bank-freeze guide](/learn/avoid-bank-freeze-when-selling-usdt) — read both
before selling anywhere, including here.
