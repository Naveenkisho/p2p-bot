## What actually moves the USDT-INR rate

USDT is designed to track the US dollar, so the rate you are quoted is
really three things stacked together:

1. **The dollar-rupee market.** The biggest component by far — when
   USD/INR moves, USDT/INR moves with it.
2. **Local demand and supply.** How much USDT Indian buyers want versus
   how much sellers are offloading shifts the rate around the forex base
   through the day.
3. **The platform's spread.** Whoever buys your USDT keeps a margin —
   always. The only question is whether it is shown honestly in the rate
   or hidden in fees.

That is the whole machine. There is no secret index; every "live rate"
you see is someone's answer to those three inputs.

## Why every platform shows a different number

Compare five platforms at the same minute and you will see five rates.
The differences are not mysteries:

- **Exchanges** show a market price, then subtract trading fees,
  withdrawal fees and 1% TDS on the trade — the headline is not what
  lands in your bank.
- **P2P marketplaces** often show the highest headline of all, because
  the stranger paying you is pricing in the risks you are about to carry
  — the [route comparison](/learn/convert-usdt-to-inr) covers what that
  premium really costs.
- **OTC desks** quote one all-in number: rate × amount = what reaches
  your account. The spread is visible right in the quote.

The professional habit: never compare headlines, compare **what lands in
the bank** — the full method is in
[how to get the best USDT rate in India](/learn/sell-usdt-best-rate-india).

## How to read any quote like a professional

Three questions sort every rate you will ever see:

1. **Is it all-in?** Ask what arrives in your account for your exact
   amount. If fees, TDS or "network charges" appear after the headline,
   the real rate is lower than advertised.
2. **Is it locked in writing?** An indicative rate that can move after
   you send your USDT is not a quote — it is bait. On this desk the INR
   figure is written into your [order](/sell) before you transfer
   anything, and it cannot change afterwards.
3. **Which payout method is it for?** UPI and IMPS can carry different
   rates and limits — a serious platform shows each method's own number,
   the way the home-page grid here does.

## Why "today's rate" matters less than a locked rate

The rate drifts all day with the dollar and local flow. Chasing the peak
of that drift is how sellers end up sending first and negotiating after —
backwards, and how [selling scams](/learn/sell-usdt-safely-avoid-scams)
usually start. The sequence that always works: check the live number,
open the order, let it lock in writing, then send. From that moment the
day's drift is the desk's problem, not yours — and the payout that
follows is from the desk's own float, with a receipt carrying the
transaction ID, which is the [clean-funds guarantee](/guarantee) working
as designed.
